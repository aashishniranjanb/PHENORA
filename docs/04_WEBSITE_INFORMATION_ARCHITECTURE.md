# PHENORA Website Information Architecture

The website is structured into six focused routes.

## Route Maps

### 1. `/` — Home (Landing Page)
- **Purpose:** 30–60 second pitch for judges and investors.
- **Key Sections:**
  - Hero (Title, Mission Statement, Explore CTA)
  - Problem Statement (AST takes too long, patient risk)
  - PHENORA Concept (Adaptive impedance intelligence)
  - Interactive V1 3D model (explore physical parts)
  - Summary Timeline (V1 -> V2 -> V3)

### 2. `/technology` — Tech Architecture
- **Purpose:** Inform technical reviewers of the underlying systems.
- **Key Sections:**
  - Explanation of electrical impedance ($Z(f) = R + jX$)
  - Block Diagram of components (Control, Test, Electrodes, AD5933, Heltec, FPGA)
  - Edge Intelligence flowchart (Filtering, Differential slope, Decision loop)

### 3. `/simulation` — Simulation Lab
- **Purpose:** The core interactive visual scientific demo.
- **Key Sections:**
  - 3D Sample chamber (medium, cells, electrodes, field lines)
  - Physics controls (conductivity, cell concentration, temperature, Rs, Rct, Cdl, growth parameters)
  - Dual graphs (Impedance vs. Frequency; delta impedance features vs. time)
  - FPGA Decision Panel (visual feedback of MEASURING -> STABLE -> STOP state)

### 4. `/research` — Literature Review & Foundation
- **Purpose:** Establish credibility with published work.
- **Key Sections:**
  - Bulk Impedance AST foundations
  - Literature matrix mapping method, measurement, and validation status
  - Research status badges (ESTABLISHED, LITERATURE-SUPPORTED, PHENORA HYPOTHESIS, FUTURE)

### 5. `/platform` — V1, V2, V3 Roadmap
- **Purpose:** Share startup roadmap without fabricating data.
- **Key Sections:**
  - V1 (Current MVP, functional prototype, physical validation)
  - V2 (Upcoming, improved sensor matrix, under development)
  - V3 (Upcoming, future integrated diagnostic platform, conceptual)

### 6. `/team` — Team & Foundation
- **Purpose:** Introduce researchers and contacts.
- **Key Sections:**
  - Core team bios, roles, and advisory contacts.
